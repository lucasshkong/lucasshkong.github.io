I am an incoming CIS Ph.D. student at the University of Pennsylvania and currently a software engineer at Hanwha Systems in Korea. I did my B.S. at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU) and was an exchange student at The Pennsylvania State University.

My research interest broadly lies in quantum computer architecture and systems. I aspire to become an independent researcher who designs fault-tolerant quantum computer systems that bridge the gap between hardware capabilities and real-world applications. Recently, I have been exploring quantum error correction.
